Photo Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Photo Cleaner for Android and iOS is built local-first. Your photos and videos are scanned and sorted entirely on your device. Their contents, file names and locations never leave your phone and are never uploaded to us. The app sends us telemetry data and crash reports only if you say yes to each of them, and both are off until you do.

The short version

  • All photo and video analysis happens on your device. We never see, upload or store your images.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location or any photo content.
  • The app asks you two separate questions, "Crash reports" and "Telemetry data". Both are off until you say yes, and you can change either one at any time in Settings.

Everything stays on your device

To do its job, Photo Cleaner needs permission to read your photo and video library. You can give it all your photos or only the ones you choose. The app then looks at your images, videos and their basic details such as size, date and dimensions, and sorts them into categories for you.

All of this, including the smart sorting step that helps group similar photos, runs locally on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Your settings, scan results, the measurements the app takes from each photo and your progress are kept only in the app's own storage on your device, and are removed if you uninstall the app.

Crash reports only with your yes

If the app crashes or something doesn't work, and you allow it, it sends us a short technical note about what went wrong, so we can fix it. Your photos are never part of it.

A crash report contains technical error details (which part of the app failed and why), basic device information (device model, operating-system version, available memory and storage, app version) and a random identifier tied to your app installation. Before a report is sent, the app removes file paths from it. It never contains your photos, videos or anything you type. Reports are sent through Google Firebase Crashlytics.

If you say no, nothing is sent. The app keeps the technical notes of the last 30 days on your phone and, after a problem, may ask you whether to send them. Older notes are deleted.

Telemetry data only with your yes

If you allow it, the app sends us a few anonymous numbers about how it's doing: how many photos were scanned, how big the gallery is, which parts of the app people use most, and where something got stuck, if it did. It helps us see what works and what needs fixing. It's never linked to your photos or to you. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

What is sent when you allow it

  • Usage events, for example that a scan started or finished, how many items were sorted into each category, how long a scan took, the app language, and whether setup was completed. These are plain numbers and fixed labels.
  • Basic device information such as device brand, model and operating-system version, so we can understand which devices the app runs on and fix device-specific problems.
  • Approximate location at country, region or city level, worked out by Google from your internet address. We do not collect GPS location.
  • An anonymous identifier that is random, tied only to your app installation, and reset if you reinstall. If you have also allowed crash reports, a crash report carries this same identifier, so we can see a problem together with the telemetry data of the same installation.

Telemetry data is sent through Google Analytics for Firebase.

What is never collected

No photo or video content, no file names, no name, email, phone number, contacts, messages, precise (GPS) location, and nothing you type. We do not link this data to your identity.

Messages from us always on

The app checks Google Firebase Remote Config for a short message we may publish (for example about an update). To answer, Google receives a random identifier tied to your app installation and basic technical details (app version, operating system, device language, country setting and time zone). No content from your phone is sent. This check does not depend on your two answers above.

Google acts as our service provider for crash reports, telemetry data and messages, and may process the data on servers in the United States. Our analytics are set up with extra privacy options on: no advertising signals and no ad personalization. Approximate location is recorded at city level or coarser, never GPS. Usage events are kept for up to 14 months.

On iPhone

Where the iPhone app works differently from the Android app:

  • Photos stored in iCloud. If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage", some originals are not on your phone. To measure those photos, the app asks Apple's Photos app to download the original to your phone, only on Wi-Fi and only while enough free space is left. The download comes from your own iCloud through Apple; nothing is sent to us, and nothing in your library is changed.
  • No tracking. The app does not track you.

If you write to us

If you send us feedback from the app, it opens your own email app with a message to us. Besides what you write and any ratings you give, the message contains the app version, the operating-system version, the device model and the app language, and you see all of it before you send. We use your email only to answer you.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Processing your media on the device is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and happens only after you grant the permission.
  • Crash reports and telemetry data rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG), given separately for each through the app's two questions and withdrawable at any time in Settings. A one-time "Send" after a problem is a consent for that one sending.
  • Messages from us rely on our legitimate interest in telling users about updates and problems (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). They contain no content from your phone.
  • Feedback emails are answered on the basis of your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction or erasure of your data;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time. For crash reports and telemetry data, just switch them off in Settings. This does not affect processing already carried out;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The authority for our location is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children

Photo Cleaner is not directed at children. It is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.
